THE LONG WAY HOME

Platinum roadmap

Play once, explore as you go, then sweep the remaining lists. Keep a manual save before each chapter: there is no mission replay.

01 / STORY

Ride the whole story

Complete Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. Mission trophies: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby!, Top Of The World, Ma, You Gotta Know When to Get Off, plus six named mission awards.

Do optional strands when they appear. No chapter trophy is reported missable.
02 / MISSABLE WATCH

Meditate early

Positive Mental Attitude requires all 18 meditation spots. PowerPyx flags a possible missable first meditation during Chapter 1; complete it when offered, then finish all remaining spots in free roam.

ONLY REPORTED MISSABLE · save before progressing past Chapter 1
03 / ROAM

Clear the checklist

After the finale, open Progress → 100% Completion. The guide reports 15 of 18 card sets count toward 100%; the trophy itself asks for 3 sets. Scan 200 vehicles, study 100 animals, finish 70 of 80 challenges, and complete the checklist’s required activities.

Free-roam remains available; most cleanup can wait until after the story.
04 / CLEANUP

Finish the odd jobs

Get 250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 14 drone-race wins, 20 distinct race wins, 100 WAiNK scans, 50 challenges and each remaining activity trophy. Check the in-game counters as you go; collect the platinum last.
